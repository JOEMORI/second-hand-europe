HOW TO UPDATE (2 minutes, keeps your saved items and watches)
-------------------------------------------------------------
Your saved items and watches belong to the folder Chrome loads the
extension from. Keep using the SAME folder and nothing is lost.

1. Find your existing extension folder (the one you picked with
   "Load unpacked" last time, probably called "gear-search").
2. Unzip the new second-hand-europe.zip somewhere temporary.
3. Copy everything inside the new "second-hand-europe" folder into
   your existing folder (keep its old name). Choose "Replace"
   when asked.
4. Go to  chrome://extensions  and click the round reload arrow on
   the extension (now called Second Hand Europe).
5. Open it again. chrome://extensions should show version 1.5.2.

Do NOT click "Remove" and load a new folder: Chrome treats that as a
new extension and your saved items and watches would be gone.
From v1.2 on you can also use "Export backup" first (since v1.5.1 it
is under Settings, the gear at the bottom of the sidebar),
just in case.



WHAT'S NEW IN v1.5.2 (compared with v1.5.1)
-------------------------------------------
- No more pop-up preview when you hover over a listing; the card
  already shows the large photo and all the details.
- Above the results, the marketplace buttons fold into one button
  ("5 marketplaces · 128 listings") once loading is done. Click it to
  filter by marketplace. Market price, bargains and load errors now
  sit on the same line.
- The 16 "more marketplaces" links fold into one button.
- "Watch: ..." on cards is now small grey text.
- Fix: Vinted conditions such as "Good" are now translated.


WHAT v1.5.1 CHANGED (compared with v1.5)
----------------------------------------
In short: a tidier interface. The sidebar keeps only what you use all
the time, help text moved behind small (i) icons, listing cards put
the photo first, and the start page shows your new listings.

- Settings: search settings (title must contain the keyword, hide
  pickup-only, eBay used only, deal marking), exclude words, backup
  and language moved into "Settings", the gear at the bottom of the
  sidebar. The gear stays at the bottom while you scroll.
- Help text: the long explanations on Browse categories, New listings,
  Saved and Watches are now behind an (i) next to the title. Hover
  over it (or tap it on a touch screen) to read.
- Listing cards: 4:3 photos, 4 per row (5 on very wide screens, 3 on
  narrow ones). The photo only shows the marketplace label (top left)
  and the save star (top right). "Below market", naive wording, no
  brand, unusually cheap and defect labels now sit next to the price.
  Place, listing time and shipping are on one line.
  Below-market listings no longer get an orange border; the label
  next to the price is enough.
- Start page: before you search, the search box is large and centred,
  with your quick searches and the 8 latest new listings from your
  watches underneath. "View all" opens New listings.
  The search box starts empty instead of repeating your last search,
  and it clears when you switch to another tab.
- The region "Nearby" is now called "Western Europe".


WHAT v1.5 CHANGED (compared with v1.4)
--------------------------------------
In short: "Browse categories" is now a catalog you pick from yourself,
and notifications show one item each, with photo and price.

- Category catalog: 40 categories in 8 groups (cameras & photo, audio &
  headphones, instruments & DJ, furniture & home, clothing, bikes,
  records & books, collectibles & finds). Each one uses the
  marketplaces' own categories (Marktplaats, 2dehands, Vinted,
  Kleinanzeigen, eBay); other countries are searched with a keyword in
  their own language.
- "My categories" are yours: add them with "Add from the catalog",
  remove any with x (press twice), including the old built-in ones.
  Removed ones can be added back at any time.
- Search the catalog in English, Chinese, Dutch or German. The right
  side shows how each ticked marketplace will be searched.
- Tick "Also watch this category" when adding to get notifications for
  it (off by default, because a whole category can be very busy).
- Can't find it? Make a custom keyword category straight from the
  search word.
- After updating you keep your current categories and custom ones.
- Notifications: one per new item, up to 3, plus one summary for the
  rest. Title = price (with local price) and marketplace, text = item
  name, small image = item photo. Clicking opens the listing; the
  summary opens New listings. In Watches you can switch to "One
  summary only" or "Only notify below market price" (keyword watches).
- Fixes: eBay is no longer skipped when you search a brand inside
  "Clothing"; a quick search keeps its category even if you removed it
  from My categories.


WHAT v1.4 CHANGED (compared with v1.3)
--------------------------------------
In short: much lighter on Chrome, search within a category, a bigger
table-style list, sold saved items leave on their own, and system
notifications even when the window is minimized.

- Chrome freezing / window that wouldn't drag while Vinted was open:
  * At most 2 Vinted pages are read at a time (about 3.5 MB each).
    Before, it could load 6 at once.
  * When Vinted's login session expires, it is refreshed only once
    every 10 minutes. Before, several searches could refresh it at the
    same time, which could make your own open Vinted tab keep reloading.
  * The invisible frames load only the page text, no photos, styles or
    ads, and read the listing data once.
  * No more minimized Vinted window. It loaded the full Vinted website
    and was the main suspect for the freezes. If Vinted can't be read,
    open vinted.nl once in Chrome and search again.
- Search within a category: pick a category next to the search box,
  e.g. "Lenses" + Schneider, or the new "Clothing" category + ARYS.
  You only get lenses (or clothes), not electrical goods. Works for
  quick searches and watches too. In "Browse categories" you can also
  type a brand or model after picking a category.
- Browse categories: the category cards shrink to a row of buttons once
  you pick one. Its price range is now separate from the search price.
- List view is now a table (inspired by HiFi Shark): large photo,
  title, marketplace, price and listing date. Click "Price" or "Listed"
  to sort. "New listings" and "Saved" can switch between grid and list
  too ("Saved" remembers its own choice and starts as a list).
- Scroll to the bottom and more results load automatically.
- Each region in the marketplace list has an "All" / "Clear" button.
- System notifications (Watches tab): turn them on or off, keep them on
  screen until dismissed, and send a test notification. They work while
  the Chrome window is minimized or closed, as long as Chrome is still
  running. On a Mac: System Settings > Notifications > Google Chrome >
  Allow notifications, style "Alerts".
- Saved items that were sold or taken down are removed automatically
  (about every 10 hours, and when you open "Saved"). The site must say
  the page is gone, or show it as sold twice at least 2 hours apart.
  Removed items stay under "Recently removed automatically" for 30 days
  with your notes; "Put back" restores one. You can switch this off.


WHAT v1.3 CHANGED (compared with v1.2)
--------------------------------------
In short: one more country, and a big round of stability fixes.

- New marketplace: Vatera (Hungary). Prices in forints are converted
  to euros. Auctions show the number of bids. If Vatera shows "blocked",
  open vatera.hu once in Chrome and search again.
- Steadier: at most 6 marketplace requests and 2 invisible frames run
  at once, and changing category or price stops the old search instead
  of letting it keep loading in the background.
- Watch checks no longer undo changes you make while a check is running
  (deleted watches coming back, new ones vanishing). Pressing "Check now"
  during a scheduled check no longer sends duplicate notifications.
- Leftover minimized Vinted windows are now closed automatically.
- eBay no longer shows 0 results when it sends a "please wait" page;
  the extension reads it another way instead.
- If a later page of a marketplace fails, the pages already loaded are
  kept, and "Load more" retries the failed page.
- Clicking the toolbar icon switches to the open extension tab without
  reloading it. Clicking a notification opens "New listings".
- Smaller fixes: "Load more" no longer disappears after a new search;
  Marktplaats "Load more" stops when there are no more priced results;
  listings seen long ago aren't announced as new again; the "Check now"
  button can't get stuck; notifications follow the language you pick;
  eBay auctions without bids say "Auction" instead of "0 bids".
- Watches: if a marketplace failed when you created a watch, or you
  changed a custom category's keywords, its first successful check now
  just records what's there instead of flooding you with notifications.
- A marketplace that doesn't respond gives up after 20 seconds instead
  of showing "Loading" forever.
- Vinted "no listing data" fixed: when Vinted's login session expires
  it shows an empty "session refresh" page; the extension now refreshes
  the session itself and reads again.
- eBay: retries once when blocked, and its listing times ("16. Oct.
  22:47") are read correctly, so "Newest first" is more accurate.
- Kleinanzeigen: adapted to its new page layout (the price could be
  taken from the description), and its times are read as German time,
  so they're right even if your computer is set to another time zone.
- One odd listing no longer makes a whole marketplace fail.
- Importing a damaged backup can no longer break the extension.
- The price filter works correctly after running a quick search or a
  watch's search; clicking a notification again always opens
  "New listings"; editing a custom category's exclude words also
  applies to its watch; the toolbar wraps on narrow windows.


WHAT v1.2 ADDED (compared with the first version, v1.0.0)
---------------------------------------------------------
More countries
- 11 new marketplaces built in: DBA (Denmark), Blocket (Sweden),
  Tori (Finland), willhaben (Austria), OLX (Poland, Portugal, Romania,
  Bulgaria), Bazos (Czechia, Slovakia), Subito (Italy).
- The marketplace list is grouped by region, with the country code in
  front of every name. Vinted and eBay sit at the top; countries near
  the Netherlands come next. Click a region to open or close it.
- Prices in DKK, SEK, PLN, CZK and RON are converted to euros at the
  European Central Bank rate, so price filters and bargain flags work
  across countries. The local price is shown under the euro price.
- More one-click links (new tab): Leboncoin, Wallapop, Milanuncios,
  Allegro, Jofogas, Njuskalo, Bolha, DoneDeal, Tradera, Huuto.net,
  CustoJusto, eBay FR/IT/ES/UK, Catawiki.

Your own categories
- "Browse categories" now has a "+ New category" card. Give it a
  name and a few keywords in any language (e.g. "hasselblad, mamiya,
  bronica"). Each keyword is searched on every ticked marketplace.
- Edit or delete it with the pencil icon. You can watch it like any
  other category.
- Built-in categories also work on the new marketplaces: where a site
  has no matching category, a keyword in its own language is used
  (e.g. "microfono" on Subito, "mikrofon" on OLX.pl).
- The "Bargain hunting" tab is now a category card called
  "Attic finds". It searches each country's language for titles like
  "old camera", "grandpa's" or "estate", sorts bargains first, and can
  be watched like any category.

More results, better prices
- Loads several pages per marketplace, and "Load more" fetches the
  next pages instead of stopping at page one.
- Search results now send the price filter to the marketplaces too
  (like categories already did), so every page is in your price range.
- Shows the total price where shipping or buyer fees are known
  (eBay, Vinted, Subito).
- "Newest first" now sorts by real listing time across marketplaces.
- "eBay: used only" switch.
- "Hide pickup-only" switch for listings you can't get shipped.
- Marktplaats / 2dehands listings marked "Bieden" (open to bids) are
  no longer dropped when you set a price. The extension opens the
  listing in the background and shows the seller's minimum bid or the
  highest bid so far; items whose minimum is above your range are hidden.

Easier to use
- Grid or list view, and a toolbar that stays at the top while you
  scroll.
- Hover over a listing to see a larger photo.
- Each time you open the extension the price range starts at "Any",
  so an old price filter never hides results. Your last keyword and
  exclude words are kept. Watches keep their own price range.
- Failed marketplaces show as one short line; click "Show why".
- English / Chinese switch at the bottom of the sidebar
  ("語言 · Language").
- Export / import a backup of saved items, watches, quick searches,
  custom categories and settings.
- New logo.

Fixes
- Vinted works again: when Vinted blocks a direct request, the
  extension reads the page in an invisible frame, so no window pops up
  when you change the price. Only if that also fails, and only while
  you are using the search page yourself, does it use a minimized
  window. Background watches never open a window.
- Changing the price on the category page no longer jumps back to the
  search results.
- eBay titles no longer show "New listing" instead of the item name.
- Category pages no longer show "below brand price" flags. Comparing
  different models of the same brand wasn't meaningful. Categories
  now only flag seller clues (no brand in the title, words like "old"
  or "grandpa"). Keyword searches still flag prices well below that
  search's median; set "Bargain flag" to Off to hide those too.
- eBay no longer fails with "403": when eBay blocks a direct request,
  the page is read in the same invisible frame.
- The invisible frames only read the page's HTML and don't run the
  site's own scripts, so they use far less memory and shouldn't slow
  Chrome down.
- Watch names follow the language you pick.

Good to know
- Converted prices are approximate; the seller's local price counts.
- Some sites (Leboncoin, Wallapop, Allegro...) block automatic reading,
  so they stay as links.

怎麼更新（約 2 分鐘，收藏和追蹤都會保留）
------------------------------------------
收藏和追蹤是跟著 Chrome 載入插件的資料夾走的，繼續用同一個資料夾就不會不見。

1. 找到你原本的插件資料夾（上次「載入未封裝項目」選的那個，大概叫「gear-search」）。
2. 把新的 second-hand-europe.zip 解壓縮到別的地方。
3. 把新資料夾裡的所有檔案複製到原本的資料夾（舊資料夾名稱不用改），問你要不要取代時選「取代」。
4. 打開 chrome://extensions，按插件（現在叫「歐洲二手搜尋」）上的圓形重新載入箭頭。
5. 重新打開插件。chrome://extensions 上應該顯示 1.5.2 版。

不要按「移除」再載入新資料夾：Chrome 會當成新的插件，收藏和追蹤就沒了。
從 v1.2 開始，也可以先按「匯出備份」以防萬一（v1.5.1 起在側欄最底下齒輪的「設定」裡）。

v1.4 跟 v1.3 差在哪（簡短版）
----------------------------
一句話：對 Chrome 的負擔大幅減輕，可以在分類裡搜尋，列表變大、變成表格，
賣掉的收藏會自己移除，視窗縮小也能收到系統通知。

- 開著 Vinted 時 Chrome 卡住、視窗拖不動：
  * 同時最多讀 2 頁 Vinted（每頁約 3.5 MB），以前最多同時讀 6 頁。
  * Vinted 登入狀態過期時，10 分鐘內只更新一次。以前好幾個搜尋可能同時更新，
    會讓你自己開著的 Vinted 分頁一直重新載入。
  * 看不見的框架只載入文字，不載圖片、樣式和廣告，資料也只讀一次。
  * 不再另外開最小化的 Vinted 視窗（它會完整載入 Vinted 網頁，是卡住的主要嫌疑）。
    讀不到時，在 Chrome 打開 vinted.nl 一次再搜尋。
- 在分類裡搜尋：搜尋框旁邊選分類，例如「鏡頭」＋ Schneider、新的「服飾」分類＋ ARYS，
  只會出現鏡頭（或衣服），不會混進電器。快捷和追蹤也能用。
  分類瀏覽選了分類後，也可以輸入品牌或型號。
- 分類瀏覽：選了分類後，分類卡片縮成一排小按鈕。分類的價格和搜尋結果的價格分開設定。
- 列表改成表格（參考 HiFi Shark）：大圖、標題、平台、價格、上架時間各一欄，
  點「價格」「上架時間」可以排序。新上架、收藏也能切換格狀 / 列表
  （收藏有自己的設定，預設列表）。
- 往下捲到底會自動載入更多。
- 平台清單每個地區可以一鍵「全選」或「清除」。
- 系統通知（追蹤清單分頁）：可以開關、設成停留到按掉，也能傳一則測試通知。
  Chrome 視窗縮小或關掉（但沒有完全結束 Chrome）也會通知。
  Mac 要到「系統設定 → 通知 → Google Chrome」允許通知，樣式選「提示」。
- 已賣掉或下架的收藏會自動移除（大約每 10 小時、打開「收藏」時檢查）。
  網站說頁面不存在，或隔 2 小時以上連續兩次顯示已售出才會移除。
  移除的商品在「最近自動移除」保留 30 天，備註也在，按「放回收藏」可以復原。
  不想要可以關掉。


v1.3 跟 v1.2 差在哪
------------------
一句話：多了匈牙利 Vatera，並修了一大輪穩定性問題。

- 新平台：匈牙利 Vatera。福林價格會換算成歐元，拍賣會標出價次數。
  如果顯示「Vatera 擋住了讀取」，在 Chrome 打開 vatera.hu 一次再搜尋。

- 追蹤檢查進行中你新增、暫停或刪除追蹤，不會再被檢查結果蓋掉；檢查中再按「立即檢查」不會重複通知。
- 沒關掉的最小化 Vinted 視窗現在會自動關閉。
- eBay 回「請稍候」頁面時不會再顯示 0 件，會改用其他方式讀取。
- 某個平台後面的頁面讀取失敗時，前面已讀到的會保留，「載入更多」會重試失敗的那頁。
- 點工具列圖示會切換到已開著的插件分頁，不會重新載入；點通知會打開「新上架」。
- 其他小修正：新搜尋後「載入更多」不會不見；設了價格時 Marktplaats 的「載入更多」會正確停止；
  很久以前看過的商品不會再被當成新上架通知；「立即檢查」按鈕不會卡住；
  通知會跟著你選的語言；eBay 沒人出價的拍賣顯示「拍賣」而不是「0 次出價」。
- 追蹤：建立追蹤時讀取失敗的平台，或自訂分類改了關鍵字，第一次讀到時只記錄現有商品，不會一次跳出一大堆通知。
- 網站沒有回應時 20 秒後放棄，不會一直顯示「讀取中」。
- Vinted「頁面裡找不到商品資料」修好了：Vinted 登入狀態過期時會先出現空白的「session refresh」頁，現在插件會自己更新登入狀態再讀一次。
- eBay：被擋時會先再試一次；上架時間（例如「16. Oct. 22:47」）會正確讀取，「最新優先」更準。
- Kleinanzeigen：配合它的新版網頁（之前價格可能誤抓描述裡的數字），時間一律以德國時間換算，電腦設成別的時區也不會算錯。
- 某一筆資料格式怪怪的，不會再讓整個平台讀取失敗。
- 匯入壞掉的備份檔不會再讓插件打不開。
- 用快捷或追蹤清單的「搜尋」之後改價格會正確生效；再點一次通知也會打開「新上架」；
  自訂分類改排除字詞會套用到它的追蹤；視窗很窄時工具列會自動換行。
